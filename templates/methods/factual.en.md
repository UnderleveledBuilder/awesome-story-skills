**What it solves**: Hand a model news, interviews, subtitles or a paper and ask for a story, and it will quietly add quotes, numbers and causes that are not in the material. "Write it like a novel" asks for scenes, dialogue, sensory detail and inner thoughts, which real material rarely contains, so the model fills the gaps with plausible content and readers cannot tell which sentence is true. Fact constraints separate telling it well from telling it right: first decide what may be used, then decide how to tell it, and after writing check every claim against the source.

**Common designs**

1. **Build the ledger first, then the structure**: before drafting, break the material into sourced items; the narrative direction is chosen only after the ledger exists, and the draft may only use ledger items. aletheia's evidence-ledger.json binds every claim, quote, person and original audio clip to a source; startup-llm-content-pipeline first has Gemini extract structured JSON, Claude writes only from it, and anything missing is written as "Not mentioned". Examples: [aletheia](https://github.com/mycyg/aletheia), [daily-bookcast](https://github.com/GODGOD126/daily-bookcast), [lawyer-short-video-skill](https://github.com/axing0916/lawyer-short-video-skill), [startup-llm-content-pipeline](https://github.com/biswajyoti-nath/startup-llm-content-pipeline)
2. **Find the conflict in the evidence**: tension is dug out of contradictions and gaps in the material, not invented. XinZhongZhiCheng-skills builds a topic where a person's own account does not match their actions, and every explanation needs two pieces of evidence, one counter-example and one alternative explanation; doodle-explainer-video requires a topic to have a verifiable counter-intuitive fact, a wrong intuitive explanation and the real mechanism, or the ending will not hold. Examples: [good-story](https://github.com/Rimagination/good-story), [XinZhongZhiCheng-skills](https://github.com/startuo/XinZhongZhiCheng-skills), [doodle-explainer-video](https://github.com/sadekxD/doodle-explainer-video), [simon-skills](https://github.com/trustfuture/simon-skills)
3. **Grade the facts**: material is layered by reliability, and inferences must be attributed or marked as uncertain. cinematic-confessional-writing separates known facts, statements by the people involved, third-party claims and unverified information; yang-jiang-restrained-narrative-skill separates what was seen first-hand, what was heard and what is guessed; yingxue-writing picks a fact mode before writing, with different rules for source-bound, personal-memory and openly fictional pieces. Examples: [cinematic-confessional-writing](https://github.com/5ysy/cinematic-confessional-writing), [yang-jiang-restrained-narrative-skill](https://github.com/constantin2088/yang-jiang-restrained-narrative-skill), [xigua-msvideo-skill](https://github.com/124zhe/xigua-msvideo-skill), [yingxue-writing](https://github.com/Jujie-YANG/yingxue-writing)
4. **Source every sentence, check against the source after writing**: sentences and numbers carry their sources, and after drafting a script or an independent reviewer checks them against the original material. data2story-skill generates a viewer.html where clicking any sentence shows the data, code or quote behind it; AutoYY's writer can only submit a candidate draft, which becomes final only after passing gates on the number of direct quotes from the material, sourced numbers and anchor coverage; ai-director-video-commentary checks the commentary against ASR timestamps and marks inferences as low-confidence for review. Examples: [data2story-skill](https://github.com/QinghongLin/data2story-skill), [AutoYY](https://github.com/DaBaoAgent/AutoYY), [ai-director-video-commentary](https://github.com/fqyy360t/ai-director-video-commentary), [voice-film](https://github.com/artkruglov/voice-film)
5. **Interview for material, let the person confirm**: life stories start with interviews, and the person decides what the facts are. lifetrace shows the person each candidate memory's original words, time, source and privacy scope, writes it to the JSON archive only after confirmation, and drafts cite entry IDs; memoir-product only does three layers of cleanup (removing filler, smoothing word order, splitting sentences) and labels anything the AI adds as "[AI补充]" (AI addition); Writingbook-AgenticWorkflow writes a story bible after 12 rounds of interviews and runs CoVe fact-checking during review. Examples: [lifetrace](https://github.com/wudixiaoshuaibi/lifetrace), [memoir-product](https://github.com/VelVet8418/memoir-product), [Writingbook-AgenticWorkflow](https://github.com/idoforgod/Writingbook-AgenticWorkflow), [wedding-video-guided-wizard](https://github.com/aaronyi97/wedding-video-guided-wizard)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- **The rounder the story, the stronger the pull to cherry-pick.** good-story cites Nature Methods: chasing a perfect narrative invites selective evidence and hidden uncertainty, so it picks the strongest framing that does not overreach rather than the most dramatic one. lawyer-short-video-skill rules that there is no reversal without a real outcome.
- **Style techniques ask for things the material does not have.** Styles in the 孙学 vein lean on precise numbers and bystander testimony. [justin-sun-skill](https://github.com/Leon-Drq/justin-sun-skill) keeps things vague or asks the user when information is missing, and does not invent numbers or bystanders for the sake of style; [jingtian-style](https://github.com/anhao/jingtian-style) writes a short post when the material is thin: "If you shared a flat, write the shared flat; don't invent fifty million to make it sound right."
- **How much invention is allowed differs from project to project.** In [agent-saga](https://github.com/bbismm/agent-saga) every event is real, and only the narrator's inner life and small textures within events are left open; [persona-story-skill](https://github.com/Dragocytus/persona-story-skill) only forbids inventing the core conflict and allows common industry detail; wedding-video-guided-wizard allows dramatized expression but not invented experiences, and guards against applying the example plot to every couple. Write this line down first.
- **Polishing brings errors back.** AutoYY runs de-AI before fact-checking and records a hash of the draft, so any change sends it through the gates again; [deepwrite](https://github.com/willyzengg-pixel/deepwrite) re-reviews whenever content has been corrected.
- **Interviews can rewrite memories too.** memoir-product lists "AI follow-up questions may plant suggestions, so the elderly person confuses the AI's prompts with their own memories" as a core risk; its countermeasure is to ask follow-ups only about what has already been said, and never to rule on discrepancies on the person's behalf.

**Where to start**: [aletheia](https://github.com/mycyg/aletheia) suits turning articles and papers into documentaries; the minimal project in its examples shows exactly how an evidence ledger is written. [data2story-skill](https://github.com/QinghongLin/data2story-skill) suits developers starting from a dataset; its seven-role pipeline and sentence-level evidence viewer come with a paper. [lifetrace](https://github.com/wudixiaoshuaibi/lifetrace) suits memoirs and biographies; its flow is interview, personal confirmation, archiving, then writing from the entries.
