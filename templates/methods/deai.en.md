**What it solves**: Fiction written by models has fixed fingerprints: stock phrases, "not X, but Y", emotions labeled outright, dialogue that sounds like a status report, paragraphs with an even rhythm. At the structural level, models like to state the theme, make cause and effect too neat, and end on the protagonist's growth. Readers can tell within a few lines. De-AI (removing AI-sounding prose) means finding these traces and fixing them, or avoiding them before writing.

**Common designs**

1. **Rule scans after writing**: turn stock phrases, sentence patterns, and punctuation into rules or scripts, scan the prose once it is saved, and fix every hit. neuro-book's llmlint has 360 rules: static rules scan the whole draft, and LLM rules judge context. novel-novel-generator adds the stock phrases newly found in each chapter to the ban list for later chapters. Examples: [neuro-book](https://github.com/notnotype/neuro-book), [novelops](https://github.com/a11003070312/novelops), [oh-story-claudecode](https://github.com/zenstory-ai/oh-story-claudecode), [novel-novel-generator](https://github.com/mike-cramblett/novel-novel-generator)
2. **Diagnosis and rewriting by layer**: fix the narrative structure first, then paragraphs, then wording. sepia does three passes based on the StoryScope paper (don't state the theme, loosen the causal chain, move reveals later → paragraph flow → surface wording). fiction-humanizer-zh rewrites summary-like plot as "setup, process, aftermath". Examples: [sepia](https://github.com/Nanako0129/sepia), [fiction-humanizer-zh](https://github.com/deedeekong07-alt/fiction-humanizer-zh), [chinese-webnovel-editor](https://github.com/fanjack510-ctrl/chinese-webnovel-editor)
3. **A gate that sends chapters back for rewriting**: de-AI is one gate on the chapter pipeline; if the counts or scores fail, the chapter goes back. xt-webnovel-writing sends the whole chapter back when it hits a "rollback-level hard gate". jarvis-write scores with stock-phrase rules plus sentence-length and paragraph statistics, rewrites the flagged parts when the score is over the limit, retests, and rolls back to the old version if the rewrite made things worse. autonovel combines mechanical scoring with LLM review, then has the model find 500 words that can be cut. Examples: [xt-webnovel-writing](https://github.com/xiaotiewinner/xt-webnovel-writing), [jarvis-write](https://github.com/ynnyh/jarvis-write), [autonovel](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel), [awesome-novel-agent](https://github.com/modoojunko/awesome-novel-agent)
4. **Positive anchors**: give the model a specific person to learn from, not just banned words. human-like-novel-workbuddy-skill injects words, sentence patterns, and rhetorical devices that people use often and models rarely use when it writes the chapter plan. loom-novel distills a writing fingerprint only from the author's hand edits. novel-style-skills distills a style.json from samples and checks for style drift after writing. Examples: [human-like-novel-workbuddy-skill](https://github.com/LittleLollipop/human-like-novel-workbuddy-skill), [loom-novel](https://github.com/WadeZhao23/loom-novel), [novel-style-skills](https://github.com/xianzl/novel-style-skills), [Chinese-WebNovel-Skill](https://github.com/Tomsawyerhu/Chinese-WebNovel-Skill)
5. **Rewriting by statistical metrics**: use metrics to find the sentences that need changing. Claude-Book computes perplexity with a local 8B model and rewrites low-perplexity sentences. novel-writer-master uses a 332-word blacklist plus statistical analysis for targeted rewrites, with the Zhuque AI detector as its benchmark. Examples: [Claude-Book](https://github.com/ThomasHoussin/Claude-Book), [novel-writer-master](https://github.com/qiuxinyuan321/novel-writer-master), [novel-humanize](https://github.com/qi531416/novel-humanize)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- Ban lists breed new habits. human-like-novel-workbuddy-skill argues that banning words only makes the model switch to its second and third choices, which over time become another kind of AI-sounding prose. novelops says that in its tests, the shorter the writing instructions, the better the prose, so it keeps only three writing instructions and leaves all prohibitions to post-writing scripts.
- Changing the wording alone is not enough. In the StoryScope study that sepia cites, after human editors rewrote the surface style, the detection rate only dropped from 95.5% to 93.9%.
- General rules can pull away from the target style. fiction-forge found that "cut adverbs, cut metaphors" moved its sample draft further from the target author, so it switched to first measuring a per-thousand-words profile of the target corpus. It also uses scanners only as a fence against problems coming back: one read-through with a state ledger found 191 problems that five rounds of automated editing had missed.
- Don't let an LLM judge whether text sounds human. In character-card-skills' blind evaluation, LLM reviewers told human writing from AI writing with only 12% accuracy.
- Rewriting can damage the plot and the characters' voices. chinese-webnovel-editor first freezes plot facts, character evidence, and world rules before touching the text. huoren-write adds no new plot facts and keeps each character's voice. Both state that getting past detectors is not their goal.

**Where to start**: [sepia](https://github.com/Nanako0129/sepia) suits people who already have a draft and want to fix it at the structural level; you can run only the diagnosis first. [huoren-write](https://github.com/laoguo2025/huoren-write) suits Chinese web novels and short drama: it changes only the wording layer, leaves the plot alone, and comes with check scripts. [fiction-forge](https://github.com/geobond13/fiction-forge) suits authors of long fiction, combining scanners, style measurement, and a full read-through.
