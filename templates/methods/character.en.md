**What it solves**: Without character profiles, the model improvises its characters on the spot in every chapter: personalities swing with whatever the plot needs, several characters' ways of speaking grow more alike the longer it runs, and characters use information they could not possibly know. Over a long text, these small deviations add up to out-of-character (OOC) behavior and characters becoming indistinguishable.

**Common designs**

1. **Motivation profiles before writing**: before writing starts, use fixed fields to spell out what each character wants, lacks, and fears, then use that to lay out the outline and beats. The fields mostly come from screenwriting theory, such as want/need and the fatal flaw, desire/fear, and Vogler's archetypes. Short drama projects also design a villain who escalates step by step as a separate task. Examples: [NovelGenerator](https://github.com/Dylin-code/NovelGenerator), [super-ai-scriptwriter](https://github.com/Doing-Tai-JI/super-ai-scriptwriter), [claude-screenwriting](https://github.com/e06084/claude-screenwriting), [short-drama](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama)
2. **Separate fixed and variable layers**: the character card is split into a read-only core (values, appearance, verbal tics, trauma) and a state updated every chapter (identity, abilities, relationships, assets). Read the card before writing; after writing, change only the variable layer and cross-check it against the timeline; during review, check for OOC against the core layer. Examples: [light-novel-creator](https://github.com/Nya-Nya-Hoshino/light-novel-creator), [ai-story-forge](https://github.com/nwiizo/ai-story-forge), [web-novel-writing-skill](https://github.com/XINGANLIU/web-novel-writing-skill)
3. **Character voice cards**: record separately how each character talks: tone, sentence endings, word habits, how they get defensive under pressure, and nonverbal actions. Inject this when writing dialogue, and check each character against it in every chapter's review. Examples: [awesome-novel-studio](https://github.com/MJbae/awesome-novel-studio), [filmgame](https://github.com/mmlong818/filmgame), [novel-novel-generator](https://github.com/mike-cramblett/novel-novel-generator), [Serendipity-Engine](https://github.com/IsaiahN/Serendipity-Engine)
4. **Characters as agents**: each important character is a separate call that gets only the information it has seen and remembers. It first decides what it will do, then a narrator or director combines the results into prose. Most common in interactive fiction and roleplay. Examples: [nest-drama](https://github.com/63435212cwu-ops/nest-drama), [gamentic](https://github.com/hec-ovi/gamentic), [Inner-Self](https://github.com/LewdLeah/Inner-Self), [AI4VisualNovel](https://github.com/ttsmallHot/AI4VisualNovel)
5. **Inferring characters from text**: feed in novels, drafts, chat logs, or public material, extract personas, tone of voice, and behavior patterns, and generate character cards or a SKILL.md for a single character. Common in fan fiction and continuations. Extraction keeps the source passages, so the author can check each item. Examples: [character-card-fiction-workflow-skill](https://github.com/shigehaomingzi1/character-card-fiction-workflow-skill), [novel-distiller](https://github.com/FutureFuzzy/novel-distiller), [soul-killer](https://github.com/Xeonice/soul-killer)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- **Decide first how much to lock**: if the core layer is fully locked, the character has no arc; if everything can change, the persona drifts. light-novel-creator opens only 8 dynamic fields for per-chapter updates and keeps the rest read-only. ai-story-forge adds a "quasi-stable layer" between the fixed core and the variable layer, which is allowed to change within limits.
- **Character voices converge as the text gets longer**: awesome-novel-studio lists "character depth collapse" as one of the three walls of long fiction. Its countermeasure is to write a tone table at design time and recheck every episode along the VOICE axis. Writing the persona once at the start is not enough.
- **Track "who knows what" separately**: a character knowing the answer too early is a different kind of continuity error, unrelated to personality. kunka-screenwriting-master has a skill dedicated to checking what each character knows. Smart-Memory stores separate memories and secrets for each character in a group chat. NPCs in project-lunar know only what they have seen with their own eyes.
- **Character autonomy does not by itself make drama**: nest-drama treats must-reach and flexible plot nodes as a gravitational field, so the plot converges through cause and effect inside the world, and adds a supervisor that judges "would a person actually do this". gamentic only lets characters ask the narrator to change the world state.
- **Don't let the model judge characters on its own**: in character-card-skills' blind evaluation, LLM reviewers agreed with each other 86% of the time, but told human writing from AI writing with only 12% accuracy; they took detail density and status bars as evidence of human writing. Whether a card is well written has to be judged by human readers, or by checks written as rules.

**Where to start**:

- [awesome-novel-studio](https://github.com/MJbae/awesome-novel-studio): suits authors writing serialized long novels. Character cards come with tone tables, and before each episode it automatically summarizes the timeline, foreshadowing, and character states from the previous two episodes. Install it as a Claude Code plugin and it is ready to use.
- [nest-drama](https://github.com/63435212cwu-ops/nest-drama): suits people who want to try "letting the characters act on their own". It runs locally on the Python standard library alone; import existing settings and you can see how its three cards per character and its information isolation work.
- [character-card-skills](https://github.com/foreverse-app/character-card-skills): suits people writing roleplay cards. It includes opening patterns, how to write a lorebook, and a deterministic detector for AI-sounding prose, and the cards it produces can be imported into SillyTavern.
