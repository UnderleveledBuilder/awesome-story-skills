**What it solves**: A draft the model writes in one pass often has problems the model can't see itself: settings contradict each other, characters know things too early, pacing collapses, stock phrases pile up. As the chapter count grows, errors also accumulate. Review and revision separate "writing" from "finding faults": independent reviewers, scores, or scripts find the problems, fixes are targeted, and the result is checked again after fixing.

**Common designs**

1. **A gate after writing, sent back if it fails**: every chapter goes through a fixed "write → review → revise → write back state" loop. Reviewers produce a list of problems by dimension, a chapter that fails is sent back, and there is a cap on rounds. Claude-Book runs three reviews (style, character, continuity) and loops at most 3 rounds. ainovel-cli's Editor reviews along seven dimensions and must quote the text as evidence for every item. Most long-novel harnesses use this structure. Examples: [inkos](https://github.com/Narcooo/inkos), [webnovel-writer](https://github.com/lingfengQAQ/webnovel-writer), [Claude-Book](https://github.com/ThomasHoussin/Claude-Book), [ainovel-cli](https://github.com/voocel/ainovel-cli)
2. **Score and select, keep only what got better**: produce several candidates at once, or rescore after a revision, and keep or discard by score. autonovel copies autoresearch's "modify → evaluate → keep or discard" loop, and each stage moves on only when its scores pass a threshold. Fibb keeps only revisions that are "measurably better". AI-automatically-generates-novels produces 3 candidates per layer; reviewers report only problems backed by quoted sentences, and the score is computed by the program from a deduction table. Examples: [autonovel](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel), [Fibb](https://github.com/Ckokoski/Fibb), [AI-automatically-generates-novels](https://github.com/wfcz10086/AI-automatically-generates-novels), [Re3](https://github.com/yangkevin2/emnlp22-re3-story-generation)
3. **A panel of reviewers with different views**: several critics with personas each look at one aspect, then a moderator combines their notes into revision instructions. CritiCS has several critics critique the plan and the prose against creative criteria such as novelty. ProseForge's three reviewers (continuity, adversarial, style) can't see each other's notes and reach a joint verdict at the end. Fibb has each major character review their own lines. Examples: [CritiCS](https://github.com/EMNLP-2024-CritiCS/Collective-Critics-for-Creative-Story-Generation), [ProseForge](https://github.com/remacheybn408-boop/ProseForge), [novel2agent-jp](https://github.com/kgmkm/novel2agent-jp), [novelforge](https://github.com/LunaRime/novelforge)
4. **Cold reads**: the reviewer does not inherit the author's context. It reads only the prose and reports whether it makes sense and whether it wants to keep reading. lengdu requires a new reader to recheck after every revision, and neither the calibration report nor the author's intent may be shown to that reader. book-genesis-studio brings in blind readers as soon as the first chapter is done, and a revised draft is kept only if the readers like it better. Examples: [lengdu](https://github.com/asass-11/lengdu), [book-genesis-studio](https://github.com/felipelobomotta-blip/book-genesis-studio), [fiction-forge](https://github.com/geobond13/fiction-forge)
5. **Deterministic checks, the author accepts**: whatever can be judged mechanically goes to scripts. The model only proposes revision patches, and the author reads the diff and decides whether to take them. story-skills records whether characters are alive or dead, foreshadowing, and who knows what in frontmatter, and its CLI reports "dead character returns" or "payoff before setup" the way a compiler reports type errors. Story-Editor has a separate editor model review only newly written content and propose memory changes with citations, which go into the store only after approval. Examples: [story-skills](https://github.com/danjdewhurst/story-skills), [StoryForge](https://github.com/XZZKANY/StoryForge), [Story-Editor](https://github.com/izanagi771-stack/Story-Editor)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- A model reviewing its own work is biased. Novel-factory puts the writer and the reviewer in two different model families and has them review blind, specifically to counter self-preference. AI-automatically-generates-novels keeps the reviewer's temperature at 0.3, on the reasoning that if the ruler itself drifts, scores can't be compared from chapter to chapter.
- Loops need an exit. autonovel stops when scores plateau, and in the first-draft stage "progress over perfection". book-genesis-studio allows at most three rounds per gate. novel_generator has a circuit breaker that forces a pause past a threshold, to prevent endless rewriting.
- Reranking candidates may matter more than editing afterward. Re3's README states that turning off the Edit module makes it much faster with little loss, while setting the candidate count to 1 and turning off reranking makes results clearly worse.
- Scripts can't catch errors that build up over time. After about 290,000 words, fiction-forge did one read-through with a state ledger and found 191 problems that five rounds of automated editing had missed, such as timeline drift and a prop appearing in two places at once. Scanners are good for keeping fixed problems from coming back, not for finding problems.
- Revising can make the draft drift further. kitten-punch-screenwriting requires fixing the layer the problem came from, and forbids rewriting the whole thing or adding new settings to fix a local problem. dsh-novel-craft's annotations change only the marked paragraphs.

**Where to start**: [Claude-Book](https://github.com/ThomasHoussin/Claude-Book) suits adding review to an existing workflow in Claude Code: the bible is read-only, state is saved per chapter, the three reviews run for at most three rounds, and the directory structure can be copied as is. [autonovel](https://github.com/NousResearch/autonovel) suits developers who want fully automatic long novels: there are separate scripts for everything from scoring the setting to revising the whole book, and it spells out how each loop stops. [lengdu](https://github.com/asass-11/lengdu) suits authors writing Chinese short fiction who care about how it reads; its core is only two steps: "independent calibration + first read by a new reader".
