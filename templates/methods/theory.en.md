**What it solves**: Models have read a lot of writing terminology, but by default they write the "average story": conflict costs nothing, scenes don't turn, and the ending wraps up with the protagonist growing and making peace. Asking the model to "write like a certain genre" usually gets a pile of catchphrases and familiar set pieces. Theory injection breaks screenwriting books, genre tropes, and platform rules into steps the model can follow and criteria that can be checked one by one.

**Common designs**

1. **A structural model as the skeleton**: first pick a structure (three-act, Hero's Journey, Save the Cat, qi-cheng-zhuan-he (kishōtenketsu), seven-point), allocate the length across its beats (Crucible splits the book into six movements by percentage), then write chapters or scenes beat by beat. Examples: [storyteller](https://github.com/johannhartmann/storyteller) (picks among six structures automatically), [The-Crucible-Writing-System-For-Claude](https://github.com/forsonny/The-Crucible-Writing-System-For-Claude) (its own 36-beat scheme with three interwoven plotlines), [NovelForger](https://github.com/cedrusdang/NovelForger), [shanyin-screenwriting-master](https://github.com/Shanyin-ai/shanyin-screenwriting-master)
2. **Screenwriting books distilled into an on-demand knowledge base**: split one book or dozens of books into topic skills or knowledge entries, and load only the relevant parts based on the request's intent, medium, and stage, so the whole body of theory does not crowd the context. Examples: [screenwriting-skills](https://github.com/jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills) (47 screenwriting books plus 23 volumes of scripts), [how-to-make-script](https://github.com/XucroYuri/how-to-make-script), [gushi-skill](https://github.com/pipivv/gushi-skill), [story-foundry](https://github.com/mmunro3318/story-foundry)
3. **Parameterized genre tropes**: most common in web novels and short drama. Load templates by genre, channel, and platform; turn payoff beats, hooks, and paywall points into ratio and placement requirements; after writing, tag each episode and count the density. Examples: [short-drama](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama) (13 genre templates, five hook types, paywall points on 10–15% of episodes), [400-duanju-shuangdian](https://github.com/icooldp/400-duanju-shuangdian), [chinese-webnovel-skills](https://github.com/tance-mang/chinese-webnovel-skills), [salt-story](https://github.com/yfge/salt-story)
4. **A cause-and-effect unit as the writing primitive**: whatever the overall structure, every event or beat is filled in using a fixed format, such as "cause → event → effect → decision", "goal → action → reaction → cost → state change", or "therefore / but". The chain is injected into the context while writing. Examples: [dramatica-flow](https://github.com/ydsgangge-ux/dramatica-flow), [super-ai-scriptwriter](https://github.com/Doing-Tai-JI/super-ai-scriptwriter), [writers-room-story-engine](https://github.com/jackterror/writers-room-story-engine), [stephen-chow-skill](https://github.com/chnjames/stephen-chow-skill) (breaks comedy into desire, rules, status, and escalation chains)
5. **Theory as a diagnostic rubric**: after writing, review the draft with checklists derived from theory, locate the problem first, then fix it in place. Examples: [claude-screenwriting](https://github.com/e06084/claude-screenwriting) (structural diagnosis), [thriller](https://github.com/pajama-studio/thriller) (clue fairness, Knox's rules check), [sepia](https://github.com/Nanako0129/sepia) (three revision passes based on the structural features of AI fiction measured in the StoryScope paper)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- **Stack every trope and you get a formula.** Everyone uses the same beat sheets and payoff-beat tables, so the outputs collide. [ty-xiaoshuo-skill](https://github.com/danieltao118/ty-xiaoshuo-skill) rules that if two chapters in a row use the same plot type, the third must switch. [story-maker](https://github.com/FURUYAN1234/story-maker) randomly combines several axes, such as theme, genre, and ending, to avoid clichés.
- **Causality that is too tidy reads as AI.** The research sepia cites lists a single plotline, neat cause and effect, a theme stated by the narrator, and an ending resolved by the protagonist's growth as structural features of AI fiction. sepia's first pass loosens the causal chain and moves reveals later. This is in tension with the "every event must have a cause and an effect" approach: the causal chain keeps the story from sprawling, but after writing you still need to check that it isn't so tidy that only one thread is left.
- **Trope libraries slide easily into copying.** [douyin-tiktok-story-skill-agent](https://github.com/liujunxibaba/douyin-tiktok-story-skill-agent) requires recombining at least three types of mechanisms and running a similarity check. stephen-chow-skill abstracts only mechanisms, stores no lines of dialogue, and runs a near-duplicate text check before the final draft.
- **Fiction theory does not transfer directly to scripts.** [write-screenplay-skill](https://github.com/egguy886/write-screenplay-skill) first strips out inner-thought description and turns emotion into actions that can be filmed. [qianqianwriting](https://github.com/catyiqian/qianqianwriting) redistributes the Save the Cat beats for episode lengths of 30–60 seconds or 1–3 minutes.
- **Check the sources yourself.** Many knowledge bases are distilled from a single book, course notes, or compilations passed around in the community. The only stated source for the methodology of [hongguo-shortdrama-screenwriting](https://github.com/zhu5859243-free/hongguo-shortdrama-screenwriting) is the author's own account. More modules do not mean better results; it is best to compare the same chapter with and without them loaded.

**Where to start**: [screenwriting-skills](https://github.com/jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills) suits film or series writing: its material covers Chinese, American, British, Japanese, and Korean screenwriting, and the matching skill triggers automatically by task. [how-to-make-script](https://github.com/XucroYuri/how-to-make-script) suits developers who want to build their own theory knowledge base; look at how it routes by request, loads a limited amount, and self-checks against rubrics. [short-drama](https://github.com/0xsline/short-drama) suits vertical-screen short drama: genre, hooks, pacing curve, and paywall points are already parameterized, so it is easy to start with.
