**What it solves**: If the prompt only says "imitate So-and-so's style", the model picks up a surface impression: it piles on a few signature words and puts on a tone, while sentence patterns, rhythm, and dialogue stay its own average habits. Over a long text the style also drifts. Style learning turns "sound like whom" into something that can be executed and checked: a rule profile distilled from samples, example passages that can be retrieved, or a set of measurable metrics. Inject them while writing; compare against them after writing.

**Common designs**

1. **Style distillation into a profile**: read samples and extract them along fixed dimensions (POV, sentence patterns, dialogue, pacing, hooks, taboos, etc.) into an installable SKILL.md or style.json that is loaded while writing. xin-novel-style-builder first cleans the first 20 chapters down to the prose, then extracts along ten dimensions. works-dna-extractor breaks a work into 16 layers of "work DNA", with an 8-dimension fit rubric. Examples: [xin-novel-style-builder](https://github.com/oscar-wang-xin/xin-novel-style-builder), [works-dna-extractor](https://github.com/Shiaoming123/works-dna-extractor), [novel-style-skills](https://github.com/xianzl/novel-style-skills), [author-style-extractor](https://github.com/alexsowake/author-style-extractor)
2. **A quantitative fingerprint, compared after writing**: profile the author with statistics such as word segmentation, sentence length, punctuation density, and TTR, then compare the finished draft against the ranges in the corpus. longzu.skillls counted all 14.65 million characters of the original novels character by character and added 45 evenly spaced close-reading points. Its audit script warns when a value falls outside the range and returns FAIL outright for misused dialogue verbs, and it can be hooked into CI. Examples: [longzu.skillls](https://github.com/zuoyou12/longzu.skillls), [novel-writer-style-cn](https://github.com/lsg1103275794/novel-writer-style-cn), [bmad-book-builder](https://github.com/jblemee/bmad-book-builder)
3. **Retrieving example passages**: instead of compressing the corpus into rules, cut it into fragments or scenes, index them, and before writing pull a few passages into the context as needed. Novel-RAG first filters out scenes with no reference value, then supports retrieval by needs such as "dialogue with a probing feel". character-card-skills picks 2–3 complementary novels as anchors for the feel of the language. Examples: [Chinese-WebNovel-Skill](https://github.com/Tomsawyerhu/Chinese-WebNovel-Skill), [Novel-RAG](https://github.com/a-big-fish/Novel-RAG), [character-card-skills](https://github.com/foreverse-app/character-card-skills), [jarvis-write](https://github.com/ynnyh/jarvis-write)
4. **Learning from the author's own edits**: instead of studying famous writers, study the author's changes to AI drafts or their ratings of them, and summarize those into personal preferences. loom-novel distills the author's hand edits into a "writing fingerprint". OpenFicM compares the AI draft with the author's final version. dsh-novel-craft has the author upvote or downvote paragraph by paragraph, and the model summarizes this into a preference profile of a few hundred bytes. word-compiler analyzes edit diffs and generates proposed rules. Examples: [loom-novel](https://github.com/WadeZhao23/loom-novel), [OpenFicM](https://github.com/tioners/OpenFicM), [dsh-novel-craft](https://github.com/shiyan688/dsh-novel-craft), [word-compiler](https://github.com/2389-research/word-compiler)
5. **Book teardown to learn structure**: what is learned is not only the prose but the reference work's structure, plot units, pacing, and tropes, which are first abstracted into narrative patterns and then applied to a new story. Most common in web novels and short drama. harnessNovel does a book teardown first, then style imitation. open-novel-fanqie tears down one benchmark book into a trope JSON, then "reskins" it to produce an angle for the new story. Examples: [harnessNovel](https://github.com/XTmingyue/harnessNovel), [open-novel-fanqie](https://github.com/kongxcer555/open-novel-fanqie), [AnySpark](https://github.com/Mikexujunrui/AnySpark), [Hermes_Script_Cool](https://github.com/way928745-collab/Hermes_Script_Cool)

**Trade-offs and pitfalls**

- Feeding in a corpus alone is not enough. After manual review, character-card-skills changed its conclusion: giving only the corpus, without banned-phrase rules, had a net negative effect. Stock phrases are held down by banning discipline; the corpus only helps with vocabulary, objects, and register.
- Profiles store patterns, not source text. dsh-novel-craft's reasoning: source text runs to thousands of characters and fills the context within two rounds, while patterns are a few hundred bytes and can be carried every round, so the model learns "how to write" rather than sentences it can copy. loom-novel learns only from the author's edits, never from the AI's own output.
- Don't average several authors. novel-style-skills distills each author separately, then combines them in layers by narrative voice, syntax, and dialogue. longzu.skillls separates the narrative skeleton two authors share from the language skins that exclude each other, and when blending them uses each skin as a complete set.
- The style can be right while the story is dead. novel-style-skills states that its style evaluation looks only at language, so a draft can pass and still have no desire, resistance, choice, or cost. harnessNovel first abstracts the reference plot into narrative patterns, to avoid simply swapping names and moving the plot over.
- Fidelity needs stated limits and blind tests. longzu.skillls notes that its rules cover only the books it sampled, and it had independent agents with local resources disabled judge authorship blind; the first round was thrown out because a reviewer had peeked at the skill.

**Where to start**: [longzu.skillls](https://github.com/zuoyou12/longzu.skillls) suits people who want to distill one author: all three layers of evidence (statistics, close reading, blind tests) are public, and the audit script has no dependencies. [dsh-novel-craft](https://github.com/shiyan688/dsh-novel-craft) suits authors who want the AI to sound more like them over time but can't say exactly what they want; it only needs upvotes and downvotes, but it depends on DeepSeek Harness. [harnessNovel](https://github.com/XTmingyue/harnessNovel) suits web novel writers who want to learn the structure of a benchmark book: book teardown and style imitation are separate stages, and there is a local web workbench.
